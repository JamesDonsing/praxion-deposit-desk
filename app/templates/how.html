How it works

The agent asks for payment. A person says yes. PayPal collects and confirms.

  1. 1
    Signed quote

    The job record holds line items, discount, region and expiry. Sample data only.

  2. 2
    Agent drafts

    The rules engine sizes the deposit. A language model, called through a server-side API, writes the invoice note and the homeowner message. Its output is schema-checked; it never sets an amount.

  3. 3
    Policy check

    Discount authority, deposit range, region caps, expiry, required fields, and whether the words match the money.

  4. 4
    Human approves

    Approve, edit or deny. The approval binds to a SHA-256 hash of the exact invoice and message. Any edit voids it.

  5. 5
    PayPal Invoicing

    Create, send and QR code through the Invoicing v2 API, with a PayPal-Request-Id so a double tap never makes two invoices.

  6. 6
    PayPal Webhooks

    Signature verified, sandbox-only, processed once. Paid, refunded and cancelled update the job, and the agent posts the next step.
